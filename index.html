Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe for families and solo female travelers?

Absolutely. Gilgit-Baltistan is one of the safest regions in Pakistan. Our guides are trained to prioritize the safety and comfort of families and solo travelers.

What is the best time to visit?

For lush greenery, May to September. For autumn colors, October is perfect. For snow lovers, December to February is best for Skardu and Hunza.
